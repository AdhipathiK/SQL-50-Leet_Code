# Managers with at Least Five Direct Reports

## Problem Statement

We have an **Employee** table that contains employee information, including their `id`, `name`, `department`, and `managerId`.

The `managerId` column stores the ID of the employee's manager. If it is `NULL`, the employee does not have a manager.

Our goal is to find the names of managers who have **at least five direct reports**.

---

## Approach

### 1. Self Join

We perform a **self join** on the `Employee` table.

- `e1` represents the **manager**.
- `e2` represents the **employee**.

The join condition:

```sql
e1.id = e2.managerId
```

matches each manager with their direct reports.

---

### 2. Group By

We group the results by the manager's ID.

This groups all employees who report to the same manager into a single group.

```sql
GROUP BY e2.managerId
```

---

### 3. Count

We use the `COUNT()` function to count the number of employees reporting to each manager.

```sql
COUNT(e1.id)
```

Since each joined row represents one direct report, the count gives the total number of direct reports for each manager.

---

### 4. Having

We use the `HAVING` clause to filter managers who have at least five direct reports.

```sql
HAVING COUNT(e1.id) >= 5
```
